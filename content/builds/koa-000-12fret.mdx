## Why 12 fret

Moving the body join from 14 to 12 frets shifts the bridge closer to the
center of the lower bout. The top is driven at its most compliant zone — fuller
bass, more complex bloom, vibrating area more evenly distributed both sides of
the bridge. The forward-shifted X bracing points at the same target. Two
choices, one acoustic intention.

## Tonewood pairing

Koa is sweet, complex, warm, and immediately musical — a slightly soft attack,
notes that develop after pick contact, bass that is round rather than extended,
treble that chimes rather than cuts. Ribbon-grain heartwood performs closer to
theoretical best than heavily figured pieces. Adirondack spruce on top adds the
articulation and headroom koa benefits from. The 000 body lets the full voice
develop while keeping the koa midrange in focus.

## Construction notes

Cross-grained Adirondack bridge plate, 2.5mm thick, CA-hardened pin holes,
edges beveled to soften the stiffness discontinuity at the perimeter. Hide
glue at every brace-to-soundboard joint, fillets left in place at the brace
feet — the gradual stiffness transition outperforms the cleaned-up sharp
junction. Bone saddle compensated for the 25.4" scale. Bone nut cut for light
phosphor bronze.
