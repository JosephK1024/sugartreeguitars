## Why bear claw

Bear claw Sitka introduces stiffness variation across the top from the figure
itself. That can produce a top with surprising character — or one that fights
its own bracing. Tap tuning through every stage matters more here than on a
plain-grain top.

## Paired comparison

Built alongside [Bocote Parlor — Adirondack Top](/builds/bocote-parlor-adirondack)
as a controlled comparison. Same body, same bracing, same construction. One
variable.
